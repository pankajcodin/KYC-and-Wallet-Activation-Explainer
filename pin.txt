KYC on Redbelly, short version. Last verified 23 October 2026.

Redbelly KYC is needed to use Redbelly mainnet: send, swap, bridge in or out, run a node and stake. It is not needed to hold RBNT on an exchange or wrapped RBNT on another chain.

Approval: usually a few minutes, in rare cases a few days.
Wallets: up to 10 per verified identity. At 10 the portal shows "Account allocation exhausted."

Two regional rules, not the same: the DAO is open to every region. Redbelly Network is not offered in 18 listed jurisdictions (terms, clause 15.1).

Official portal only: https://access.redbelly.network/
Identity problems: https://averer-help.freshdesk.com/support/tickets/new
Never share your seed phrase or private key.
