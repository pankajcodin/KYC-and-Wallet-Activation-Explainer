Clause 15 heading: "Services not offered in certain jurisdictions"

15.1  We do not offer our services and do not carry on business in the following jurisdictions:
      Afghanistan; Central African Republic; Democratic People's Republic of Korea;
      Democratic Republic of the Congo; Guinea-Bissau (the terms page spells it "Guinea-Bussau");
      Iran; Iraq; Lebanon; Libya; Myanmar; Russia; Somalia; South Sudan; Sudan; Syria; Ukraine; Yemen; Zimbabwe.
      That is 18 jurisdictions.

15.2  Users that are tax residents or located at the jurisdictions mentioned in clause 15.1 are not
      allowed to access or use the website, platform, and software suite and tools.

15.3  The user represents and warrants that:
      (a) they are not located in a country subject to a United States government embargo, or designated
          by the United States government as a "terrorist supporting" country; and
      (b) they are not listed on any United States government list of prohibited or restricted parties.

15.4  If the user breaches clause 15, Redbelly may suspend or terminate access to the website and/or
      platform without prior notice, and the user releases Redbelly from related claims.
