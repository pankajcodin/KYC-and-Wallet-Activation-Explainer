## What the proposal asked for (summary)

1. Remove the whitelist requirement for DAO voting, where technically applicable.
2. Remove geographic restrictions for General DAO Members.
3. Keep all existing geographic limits for High Council Members and General DAO Leadership.
4. Update the Constitution and related governance documents, and publish them.

Section "Decision Requested" lists these four items. Milestone 2 says geographic restrictions would apply only to High Council Members and General DAO Leadership.

## Limits of this evidence

The vote shows the DAO approved the proposal. It does not show whether the Constitution and the Handbook have been updated yet. The proposal changes the DAO's own membership rule. It does not mention Redbelly Network's terms, so clause 15 of the terms is not affected by it.
